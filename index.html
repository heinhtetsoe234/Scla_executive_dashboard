Definitions and data notes
  • Apartments downloaded (shown as registered) is the cumulative count of unique apartments on the app. Apartments paying counts unique apartments that paid a bill through the app during the month. Unpaid and unregistered are the remainder of total apartments.
  • Cumulative residents and app users add up the monthly new users from the first month in the data. They use the September 2026 data report, which restates a few earlier months slightly.
  • October 2025 to March 2026: total apartments and zone detail were not in the source files, so unpaid and unregistered counts start in April 2026.
  • June 2026: zone figures come from the Data_Detail tab. The Property Detail tab in that file repeats July's flags, and the unregistered table there does not reconcile to total apartments.
  • May 2026: the management report shows 3,197 downloaded apartments; the summary tab in the data report shows 3,199. The dashboard uses 3,197, which matches the property-level list.
  • To add a month, append a row to the Monthly sheet (and zone rows to the Zones sheet) in SCLA_Dashboard_Data.xlsx, then choose Update data. The newest month becomes the default.
